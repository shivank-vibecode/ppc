# Project Griffin Marketing Strategy Review

**Date:** 2026-07-07
**Source:** Plaud recording (Summary)

---

**Attendees:** Shivank Shankar, Naomi, Speaker 3, Speaker 4, Speaker 5

**Executive Summary:**
Comprehensive strategy session to redefine the paid marketing approach, which is currently underperforming, particularly for the 2025 cohort. Core discussion: abandoning the "cohort ROAS" model and identifying a new North Star metric to guide budget allocation and performance evaluation. Also covered a full-funnel (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU) strategy for MT5 with a proposed $100K month-one budget, a 2024-vs-2025 performance post-mortem, automating cohort analysis into a reusable "skill" via platform APIs, and an immediate directive to stop affiliates bidding on brand keywords.

**Topic: Defining a North Star Metric**
FTDs dismissed as a poor value indicator; 30-day LTV criticised as a poor proxy for the 6-month break-even cycle. A cohort-based ROS model was proposed but seen as slow to validate and hard to action day-to-day. Team leaned toward "month-on-month absolute revenue increase" (conditional on positive ROS) as a more direct measure, while also considering a dual-metric approach: non-cohort revenue for budgeting/reporting, cohort revenue for monitoring user quality and long-term optimisation.
Decision: the current cohort-ROAS model is flawed and will be replaced; final North Star pending further data analysis.
Actions: Shivank to lead finalisation of the North Star metric and test the cohort revenue formula on historical BOFU data; Naomi to compile cohort and non-cohort data for AI-assisted strategy analysis.

**Topic: 2024 vs. 2025 Cohort Performance & Strategy Reset**
2024 cohorts were profitable (recouping spend in 8–14 months); 2025 cohorts are significantly underperforming, with a marked drop-off starting around July 2025, coinciding with when another team began influencing spend decisions. A "post-mortem" is needed covering team/strategy changes, product changes, and market shifts. Proposal to pause all campaigns for 2–3 months and rebuild a full-funnel strategy from scratch, removing the cohort concept and related integrations.
Decision: a detailed analysis of the 2025 decline is top priority; further planning is blocked pending a performance dashboard from Jatin.
Actions: Shivank to urgently follow up with Jatin for the year-on-year dashboard, then run a deep-dive comparison with Naomi.

**Topic: MT5 Full-Funnel Budget and Campaign Strategy**
Naomi presented a $100K month-one budget for an MT5 full-funnel strategy across three streams: MT5 full-funnel, current campaigns (~$104K maintenance), and a generic non-MT5 full-funnel. Outcome projections need to clarify the 6-month lag for cohort-based revenue realisation.
Decisions: MT5 outcome slides must state the 6-month revenue-realisation lag; budget plan must include rationale/logic columns and audience-sizing estimates by stage/GEO.
Actions: Shivank and Naomi to clarify timelines and add rationale columns to the MT5 deck; Shivank to decide on budget consolidation and confirm the MT5 execution team lead.

**Topic: Methodology, Attribution, and Automation**
Naomi's manual cohort-analysis process classifies campaigns into tiers (proven growth, maintenance, declining ROAS). Discussed an assisted-conversion attribution percentage for TOFU/MOFU activities (previously ~10%, likely should be higher at 30–40%). Proposed building a reusable "skill" to automate campaign analysis via platform APIs (Google, Bing) for deeper data. Also proposed testing value-based custom audience lists on Google/Meta.
Actions: Shivank and Naomi to align on an attribution percentage; Shivank to define the analytics "skill" pipeline and research custom-audience-list implementation.

**Topic: Immediate Tactical Actions and Spend Guardrails**
Naomi connected poor performance partly to affiliates bidding on brand keywords and gave an immediate directive to stop this. Proposed a new guardrail tying spend directly to a minimum ROAS (e.g. 200–300%) to limit spend on underperforming campaigns. Current spend (₹500K) agreed to be unsustainable and due for reduction.
Decisions: affiliates must stop all brand-keyword bidding immediately; marketing spend will be reduced; a ROAS-based spending guardrail agreed in principle.
Actions: Naomi to instruct Prakash to inform affiliates and set up a violator-blocking process; Naomi and Shivank to further define and document the ROAS-based guardrails.
