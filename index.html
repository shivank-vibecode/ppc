Root Cause Overview

29 Jun 2026
What Happened
  • We’re adding 62% more users than last month. But first-time trades dropped 72% — from 130/day to 36/day. Users are coming in and getting stuck before they can trade.
  • The funnel tells us exactly where: signups are healthy (+19%), but the moment users try to deposit, 41% fewer make it through. And of those who deposit, 26% fewer complete a trade. The break is at the money step.
  • The likely cause: app crash rate is 88.7% on Android and 83.5% on iOS (industry standard is 99.5%+). That means ~5,700 users hit a crash daily — and the flagged versions (v1.9.0, v1.10.2) correspond to the deposit and trade screens. Something broke around Jun 27–28 that made this much worse overnight.
  • This affects everything. Paid campaigns ($331K spent) are pushing users into the same broken funnel. Organic users who used to convert for free are now failing at the same step. Until the deposit/trade experience works, nothing downstream — campaign optimization, scaling, new channels — will move the needle.
So What
  • One fix unblocks everything. If deposit conversion returns to 22.6% (where it was 30 days ago), we recover ~63 organic trades/day without spending a dollar more. That’s ~$136K/month in revenue currently being lost.
  • The same fix also rescues paid. Paid users hit the identical broken step, so paid CAC drops ~40% mechanically once the funnel works. We don’t need to restructure campaigns to see that improvement.
  • Meanwhile, retargeting is already proving the model works: $59 spent, $10.5K returned (178x ROAS). Dormant users who bypass the broken onboarding flow are converting. This confirms the product works — the issue is isolated to the deposit/trade step for new users.
  • Decisions needed: (1) Diagnose what broke on Jun 27–28 at the deposit/trade step — 24h. (2) Cut Google budget from $23K to $5K/day until the funnel recovers — saves ~$18K/day. (3) Switch Facebook from install optimization to deposit optimization — 48h.
Blindspots
  • DAU (the north star) is rising while revenue drops. Every decision made — cheap Facebook installs, Google scale-up, brand defense — was consistent with hitting DAU. The metric may be driving the wrong behavior.
  • We have zero KYC and payment gateway data. If the blocker isn’t an app crash but a failed identity check or a declined payment, the fix is completely different. We need this data within 48h.
  • No deploy or release log for Jun 27–28. Trades dropped 36% overnight on that date. Without knowing what shipped, we’re guessing at the cause.
Phase Assessment
  • This app is in Phase 1: Signal Hunt. MMP is live (AppsFlyer), first paid dollars are flowing ($331K in 4 weeks), but there is no single channel-geo pair with validated LTV:CAC >2:1 at N≥200 FTDs. We are still searching for what works.
  • Phase 1 means we should be optimizing for learning rate — which channel-geo pairs produce traders? Instead, budget was scaled 5x before this question was answered. That is the core strategic error.
  • Critical blocker: We cannot evaluate any channel because the app funnel is broken at the deposit/trade step (Wallet→FTD −41%). Until this is fixed, all signal is noise. Phase 1 learning is paused.
