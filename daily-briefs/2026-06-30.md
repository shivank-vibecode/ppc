## Part 1: What the Data Shows

### Finding 1: Facebook is targeting Bangladesh — a zero-FTT geo
**[HIGH confidence]**

Geo data reveals 60% of Facebook's Jun 29 volume (4,256 of 7,019 installs) goes to Bangladesh. Over the full period, Bangladesh received 10,114 installs from Facebook and produced exactly zero first trades. Not one.

Other Facebook geos (Algeria: 1,656 installs/0 FTT, Congo: 1,640/2, Morocco: 556/0, Egypt: 546/0, Iraq: 462/0) show the same pattern. Facebook is running high-volume campaigns in geos where Deriv has no trader base.

Audience Network: 3,814 installs, 0 FTTs. This is pure waste regardless of any cost calculation.

**DAU impact:** Zero. Facebook is producing app openers who never trade and likely never return.

### Finding 2: Google spend scaled into the wrong campaigns
**[HIGH confidence]**

Google spend data is now available:

| Period | Daily Spend | Daily FTTs | Cost/FTT |
|--------|-------------|------------|----------|
| Jun 8–16 | $2,506–5,421 | 22–59 | $83–246 |

When Google scaled, budget shifted to search campaigns: brand keywords ($32K, 8 FTTs), login ($42K, 13 FTTs), chart patterns ($25K, 4 FTTs), multi_asset ($41K, 0 FTTs).

### Finding 3: Organic conversion is collapsing
**[MEDIUM confidence]**

Organic installs ("None" campaign) are steady at 3,100–3,600/day. But organic FTTs:
- Jun 1–16: 82–123 FTTs/day (2.5–3.5% FTT rate)
- Jun 23–28: 48–77 FTTs/day
- Jun 29: 26 FTTs (0.75% FTT rate)

This is a 70–79% decline in organic trader output with no change in install volume. Possible causes:
1. **App crashes** (83–89% crash-free) killing the signup/real-account flow
2. **Product change** breaking onboarding between Jun 17–18
3. **Bot traffic** inflating organic install counts
4. **Facebook cannibalization** — users who would have installed organically seeing FB ads instead

The timing (decline begins around Jun 17–18) coincides with both Google's spend scaling and the start of organic FTT decline. A product or app-stability issue is the most likely common cause.

### Finding 4: QR install quality is also declining
**[MEDIUM confidence]**

QR installs (deriv-home-mobile-app-qr-install) remain at 3,200–4,260/day but FTT rate dropped:
- Jun 24–26: 0.25–0.54% FTT rate
- Jun 29: 0.18% FTT rate (6 FTTs from 3,421 installs)

QR installs are not subject to paid ad targeting issues. If QR conversion is declining, the problem is downstream — in the app itself.

### Finding 5: DAU is surging on vanity installs
**[HIGH confidence]**

AppsFlyer DAU: 46,621 on Jun 29, up 63% from 28,530 on Jun 23. But paid channels produced only 5 traders on Jun 29. Organic produced 26. The DAU number is growing because Facebook is pumping in users who open the app once. Retention data will determine if this DAU holds.

## Part 2: What Would Sharpen This

### Facebook spend
**What:** Daily campaign-level spend from Facebook Ads Manager (Date, Campaign Name, Amount Spent, Currency, Geo).
**Why:** Facebook quality is definitively terrible (0.03% FTT, Bangladesh-heavy). But we cannot calculate cost-per-FTT or confirm the budget recommendation without spend. At any plausible CPI, the math will confirm "pause," but the data needs to say it.
**Where:** Facebook Ads Manager → Export → Campaign-level delivery report, Jun 1–29.

### Organic conversion drop diagnosis
**What:** (a) Firebase Crashlytics crash data segmented by screen/funnel step (signup screen, real-account creation, etc.). (b) Product changelog for Jun 15–29. (c) Daily organic install counts from Google Play Console.
**Why:** Organic FTT declined 70–79% despite stable install volume. This is either an app-level issue (crashes at a specific funnel step), a product change, or bot traffic. Each hypothesis requires different data to confirm.
**Where:** (a) Firebase Crashlytics → Filter by screen. (b) Internal product team. (c) Google Play Console → Statistics → Installs.

### Retention by source
**What:** Day 1/7/30 retention rates segmented by media source, from AppsFlyer Cohort Report.
**Why:** Current DAU surge may be a one-week spike. If Facebook users have 0% Day-7 retention, DAU will drop next week. If Kenya/Google users retain at 20%+, they are building durable DAU.
**Where:** AppsFlyer → Cohort Report → Split by Media Source.

### Attribution window confirmation
**What:** Current AppsFlyer attribution window settings (lookback window, re-engagement window) for the Google FTDs campaign.
**Why:** 178 installs producing 364 FTTs is structurally impossible. Until attribution is understood, Google's aggregate numbers cannot be trusted. Per-campaign rates (3–8%) remain valid.
**Where:** AppsFlyer → App Settings → Attribution.
