### Finding 3: Organic trader output collapsed but organic DAU is stable
**[MEDIUM confidence — root cause unclear]**

Organic installs are steady at ~3,100–3,600/day. Organic DAU grew from 16,040 to 19,555. But organic FTTs:

| Period | Organic FTTs/day | FTT Rate |
|--------|------------------|----------|
| Jun 1–16 | 82–123 | 2.5–3.5% |
| Jun 17–22 | 62–109 | declining |
| Jun 23–28 | 48–77 | 1.5–2.4% |
| Jun 29 | 26 | 0.75% |

This is a 70–79% decline in organic trader output with no decline in installs or DAU. The funnel is breaking somewhere between install and first trade for organic users too.

Hypotheses:
- **(a) App crashes** — crash-free rates at 83–89% mean 5,100–7,900 users crash daily. If crashes disproportionately hit the signup or real-account creation screens, they would suppress FTTs for all sources equally.
- **(b) Product change** — the decline starts around Jun 17–18. A product deployment around that time could have broken the onboarding flow.
- **(c) Increased low-quality organic traffic** — Facebook's heavy spending in Bangladesh and Africa may be generating organic spillover (users who see an ad, don't click, but later install organically).

Need: Firebase Crashlytics data segmented by screen/funnel step + product changelog for Jun 15–29.

### Finding 4: QR code is the standout channel
**[HIGH confidence]**

QR code metrics:
- DAU: 14,083 (doubled from ~7K in two weeks)
- FTTs: 6–23/day (declining but positive)
- Cost: $0
- DAU share: 30%

QR users scan a physical or digital code with intent — they are not algorithmically targeted. This channel is growing organically and producing meaningful engagement. The FTT decline may be an app-stability issue (same as organic) rather than a QR-specific problem.

QR is the only channel where both DAU and install volume are growing simultaneously.

### Finding 5: Apple Search Ads is negligible
**[HIGH confidence]**

Apple Search Ads: 1–52 installs/day, 0 FTTs across the entire period. Total spend: ~$4,300. DAU contribution: 59 users. This channel is not at a scale where it can be meaningfully evaluated, but it is producing zero traders.

## Part 2: What Would Sharpen This

### 1. Facebook Ads Manager spend export
**What:** Daily campaign-level spend (Date, Campaign Name, Amount Spent, Currency, Country).
**Why:** Facebook quality is definitively terrible (0.03% FTT, Bangladesh-heavy). But we cannot calculate cost-per-FTT or cost-per-DAU. The "pause Facebook" recommendation is HIGH confidence on quality but incomplete without cost.
**Where:** Facebook Ads Manager → Export → Campaign-level delivery report with geo, Jun 1–29.

### 2. Organic FTT decline root cause
**What:** (a) Firebase Crashlytics crash data segmented by screen/funnel step (signup, real-account creation, deposit). (b) Product changelog for Jun 15–29 — any app deployments, A/B tests, or onboarding flow changes. (c) Bot detection data from AppsFlyer Protect360.
**Why:** Organic FTT dropped 70–79% with stable installs. This is the most alarming finding because organic is the foundation of the business. Three hypotheses exist; each requires different data to confirm.
**Where:** (a) Firebase Console → Crashlytics → Filter by screen. (b) Internal engineering/product team. (c) AppsFlyer → Protect360 → Invalid Activity Report.

### 3. Retention by media source
**What:** Day 1/7/30 retention rates by media source from AppsFlyer Cohort Report.
**Why:** Facebook produced 7,749 DAU on Jun 29. If Day-7 retention is near zero, this DAU will evaporate. If QR and organic users retain at 20%+, they are building durable DAU. This single dataset would confirm whether the DAU surge is real or a one-week spike.
**Where:** AppsFlyer → Cohort Report → Split by Media Source → Retention Day 1, 7, 30.

### 4. Google campaign-level geo breakdown
**What:** Google Ads campaign performance broken down by country (campaign × geo × date).
**Why:** We know Kenya is $47/FTT and Colombia is $8,314/FTT at the source-geo level. But we need campaign × geo to know which specific campaigns are running in which geos, so we can surgically pause the wasteful ones without killing efficient geo targeting.
**Where:** Google Ads → Campaigns → Segment by Country → Export.
