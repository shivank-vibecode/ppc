Jun 30 is Facebook's worst FTT rate day since launch (0.069%). The main campaign (`row-prosp-cpa-mob-app_installs-0626-en-fb-android`) produced 7,107 of the 7,240 installs. The Audience Network variant has been effectively paused (5 installs, down from 2,612 on Jun 24).

**Deriv-App-Promo-June-Android** (attributed under Facebook's media source): 128 installs, 0 FTTs. This is a separate campaign also producing nothing.

**DAU impact:** Facebook DAU continues to inflate total numbers but produces ~0.7 FTTs per 1,000 DAU. In contrast, organic produces ~10 FTTs per 1,000 DAU.

## Part 2: What Would Sharpen This

### Google campaign optimization — currently HIGH confidence on waste, MEDIUM on fix
- **Data needed**: Google Ads campaign × geo × device breakdown for Jun 18–30 (CSV: Date, Campaign, Country, Device, Cost, Conversions, Conversion Value)
- **Where to get it**: Google Ads → Campaigns → Segment by Country and Device → Download
- **What it unlocks**: We know Google Kenya ($47/FTT from prior analysis) is efficient but can't see which campaigns are running where. This data would allow surgical budget reallocation rather than broad pauses.

### Facebook spend data — still missing
- **Data needed**: Facebook Ads Manager daily campaign-level spend with geo and placement (Date, Campaign Name, Country, Placement, Amount Spent, Currency)
- **Where to get it**: Facebook Ads Manager → Export → Campaign-level delivery report
- **What it unlocks**: We can calculate actual cost-per-FTT for Facebook. At 0.069% FTT rate, even a $0.10 CPI implies $145/FTT. This would definitively prove the channel is uneconomical.

### Dynamicworks attribution
- **Data needed**: What is the `dynamicworks` media source? Is it an affiliate network, a partner, or an internal channel?
- **Where to get it**: Internal marketing ops / partnership team
- **What it unlocks**: 218 installs with 2 FTTs at zero visible cost is potentially interesting. If this is a new affiliate producing traders, it should be monitored and possibly scaled.
